Piotr Spychalski, MD, PhD

Colorectal surgeon and clinical researcher · University Clinical Centre and Medical University of Gdańsk, Poland · Publications · Polski (informacje dla pacjentów)

Assistant Professor at the Department of Surgical Oncology, Transplant and General Surgery, Medical University of Gdańsk (Gdański Uniwersytet Medyczny), and general surgery specialist in the colorectal surgery unit of the University Clinical Centre in Gdańsk. Scientific coordinator of the Laboratory of Research in Organ Medicine at the Medical University of Gdańsk.

Clinical practice

  • Colon and rectal cancer: assessment and surgical treatment at the Colorectal Cancer Treatment Centre of the University Clinical Centre.
  • Robotic and laparoscopic colorectal surgery: operations using the da Vinci system and laparoscopic technique.
  • Early colorectal cancer: organ-preserving treatment within international clinical trials (ECOPOP project).
  • Diverticular disease: surgery for recurrent and complicated disease.
  • Stoma surgery: stoma formation, restoration of bowel continuity and management of complications, including high-output stoma.
  • Proctology: including haemorrhoidal disease.

Clinical trials

Trials open to patients at the University Clinical Centre in Gdańsk:

  • ETHOS: early colon cancer; endoscopic full-thickness resection compared with surgery. National Principal Investigator for Poland. NCT06940947
  • SCAR: cancer found after polyp removal; completion endoscopic treatment compared with surgery. NCT06057350
  • STOP-HOS-1: prevention of high-output stoma after ileostomy formation. NCT06917963

Recruitment completed: PRISTINE, preventive negative pressure wound therapy compared with primary closure after emergency laparotomy for peritonitis (Principal Investigator; results being analysed; NCT05684198).
Ongoing: CORAL, comparing robotic and laparoscopic colon resection (Principal Investigator; funded by the Polish Medical Research Agency, 2026 to 2031).

Research

  • Colorectal cancer screening and colonoscopy quality: co-author of the NordICC randomised trial and of analyses from the Polish Colonoscopy Screening Program (adenoma detection rate, BMI and colorectal cancer risk, screening safety).
  • Organ-preserving treatment of early colorectal cancer: project lead at the Medical University of Gdańsk and co-applicant of the Horizon Europe project ECOPOP (grant 101156165, coordinated by the University of Oslo, 16 partners), which runs three randomised trials: ETHOS (NCT06940947), SCAR (NCT06057350) and T-REX (NCT07669298).
  • Comparative-effectiveness trials in colorectal surgery: Principal Investigator of CORAL (COlon Resections: Assessing Robotic And Laparoscopic approach in textbook outcomes), funded by the Polish Medical Research Agency (ABM/2025/2).
  • Perioperative outcomes: Principal Investigator of PRISTINE, negative pressure wound therapy after emergency laparotomy (NCT05684198), and of two studies of continuous glucose monitoring: CLUE after emergency laparotomy (NCT05641792) and CGM-CRC in colorectal cancer surgery (NCT06011473); STOP-HOS-1 trial of intensified omeprazole to prevent high-output ileostomy (NCT06917963); co-author of the Polish recommendations on prehabilitation (2023).
  • Industry-sponsored studies: WoundWatch (WW001; sponsor CutoSense Oy), overall Principal Investigator, completed and published (NCT05805137); 09 AP ONCON (sponsor AronPharma), site Principal Investigator, completed and published (NCT06017661).
