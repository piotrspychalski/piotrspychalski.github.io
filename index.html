Piotr Spychalski, MD, PhD

Colorectal surgeon and clinical researcher · Medical University of Gdańsk, Poland · Publications · Informacje dla pacjentów (PL)

Assistant Professor at the Department of Surgical Oncology, Transplant and General Surgery, Medical University of Gdańsk (Gdański Uniwersytet Medyczny), and general surgery specialist in the colorectal surgery unit of the University Clinical Centre in Gdańsk. Scientific coordinator of the Organ-Specific Medicine Research Laboratory at the Medical University of Gdańsk.

Research

  • Colorectal cancer screening and colonoscopy quality — co-author of the NordICC randomised trial and of analyses from the Polish Colonoscopy Screening Program (adenoma detection rate, BMI and colorectal cancer risk, screening safety).
  • Organ-preserving treatment of early colorectal cancer — co-applicant and site investigator in the Horizon Europe project ECOPOP (grant 101156165), which runs three randomised trials: ETHOS, SCAR and T-REX.
  • Comparative-effectiveness trials in colorectal surgery — Principal Investigator of CORAL (COlon Resections: Assessing Robotic And Laparoscopic approach in textbook outcomes), funded by the Polish Medical Research Agency (ABM/2025/2).
  • Perioperative outcomes — STOP-HOS-1 trial of intensified omeprazole to prevent high-output ileostomy (NCT06917963), PRISTINE trial of negative pressure wound therapy after emergency laparotomy (NCT05684198), continuous glucose monitoring in colorectal cancer surgery (NCT06011473), prehabilitation.
